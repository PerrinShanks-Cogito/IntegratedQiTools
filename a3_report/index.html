      What an A3 report is: a way of telling the story of an improvement project on one sheet of A3 paper, from the problem and its root causes to the changes tested and how they will be sustained. Keeping it to one page makes you focus on what matters, and makes it easy to share on a ward, at a huddle or at a poster event.

      Adding charts and pictures: build a chart in any FOCUS tool, such as a run chart, SPC chart, Pareto chart or fishbone diagram, then choose Export and Add to A3 report. Use Add a picture, or paste, for anything else. Charts from the tools are kept in this browser on this computer until you delete them. Adding the same chart again updates it rather than adding a copy, and the list offers to remove charts already on the report and charts over 3 months old. Everything placed on the report is saved in its project file, so use Save to keep it and open it again on another computer.

      Fitting it on the page: the report shares out the space between sections. When there is too much to fit, it makes the charts smaller first, then the text, down to 9pt. If it still doesn't fit, it tells you which column to shorten. Export gives you an editable A3 PowerPoint, an A3 PDF (through your browser's print window) or a PNG image for slides.
