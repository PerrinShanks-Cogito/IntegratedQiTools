Build a 2x2 matrix

Score each item on two measures and see them on a 2x2 grid, such as change ideas by ease and impact, or stakeholders by influence and interest. Use Data sheet to download a sheet for your team to list and score items in. Everything runs in your browser, so your data is never uploaded.

Result

Paste your scored items on the left to build your matrix.

How to read it: each dot is one item, placed by its two scores. The dashed lines split the grid into four groups, and the tool lists the items in each with what to do about them. Choose the matrix type that fits your question: ease and impact to pick change ideas, influence and interest to plan how to involve stakeholders, confidence and impact to decide which ideas to test with PDSA cycles and which are ready to implement, urgency and importance to plan project tasks, likelihood and consequence for project risks, and importance and performance to find where patient or staff feedback says to improve first. Or name your own axes.

Scoring well: agree as a team what a low and a high score mean before you start, and score together, or each separately and then talk through big differences. The scores are judgments, so use the matrix to guide the conversation rather than to settle it. For formal risk management, use your organisation’s risk register and its 5 by 5 risk scoring.
