SPC chart builder

Paste your data to get an XmR chart with control limits, special cause signals and a plain-English reading of what the chart shows, following NHS England's Making Data Count approach.

Calculated in your browser. Nothing you paste is uploaded or saved.

Your data

Paste one value per line, or a label and a value (dates, weeks, anything). A header row is fine.

About the measure

Control limits

To recalculate limits after a known change, click that point on the chart, or use the table of values.

Special cause rules

A single point outside the limits is always a signal. Defaults follow NHS England's Making Data Count guidance.
