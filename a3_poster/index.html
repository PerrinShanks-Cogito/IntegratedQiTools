      What an A3 is: a way of telling the story of an improvement project on one sheet of A3 paper, from the problem and its causes to the changes tested and what happened. Keeping it to one page makes you focus on what matters, and makes it easy to share on a ward, at a huddle or at a poster event.

      Adding charts: build a chart in any FOCUS tool, such as a run chart, SPC chart, Pareto chart or fishbone diagram, then choose Export and Add to A3 poster. Charts are kept in this browser on this computer until you delete them. Charts placed on a poster are saved in the poster's project file, so use Save to keep the poster and open it again on another computer.

      Fitting it on the page: the poster shares out the space between sections. When there is too much to fit, it makes the charts smaller first, then the text, down to 9pt. If it still doesn't fit, it tells you which column to shorten. Use Export and Print or save as PDF for an A3 PDF, or the PNG image for slides.
