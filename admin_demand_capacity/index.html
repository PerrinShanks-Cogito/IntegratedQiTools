Admin demand and capacity model

Enter your admin team’s work streams, how long each task takes, weekly demand and staff to see how many hours and how much staff (WTE) you need, which roles are short, and how long any backlog will take to clear. Follows the NHS England demand and capacity approach, measured in units of time.

Calculated in your browser. Nothing you paste is uploaded or saved.

1Set-up

Required capacity is shown as a range from this percentile to the 85th, as NHS England recommends. Choose a higher value for more variable or more urgent work.

Time each full-time person is away in a year, in working days:

Use your team’s own sickness rate, and an activity audit to measure time not spent on tasks if you can. List regular meetings and admin as fixed work in step 5 rather than here.

2Work streams

Columns: work stream, minutes per task (the mean), the role that does it, turnaround target in working days (0 for same day), the share that is failure demand (chasers, duplicates, rework) and the number of tasks waiting now. Only the first two are needed.

3Timed observations (optional)

One row per timed task: the work stream (named as in step 2) and the minutes it took. You can paste an activity log export with headings such as ActivityType and DurationMinutes.

4Weekly demand

One row per week: the week start date, then a column for each work stream with headings that match step 2. Count tasks arriving (emails received, calls offered, letters dictated), not tasks done, so unmet demand isn’t missed.

Required capacity is based on demand in the baseline. Pick a stable period of 10 to 52 weeks. Click a point on the SPC chart to leave an unusual week out.

5Staff and fixed work

One row per post: name, role (as in step 2) and WTE. Leave vacant posts out, or the gap will be understated.

Regular work that doesn’t depend on demand, such as meetings, 1-1s, reporting and MDT preparation. Minutes per week for the whole role, not per person.

6Scenarios

For example 10 for a 10% rise, or -5 for a 5% fall.

For example 50 if auto-replies, better information or fixing the cause would stop half of the chasers and rework.
