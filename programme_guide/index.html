      Every document, by stage

      Documents are numbered by FOCUS stage, in the order you usually need them. Choose one to see what it is for, what it draws on, and the filled-in example; the documents it draws on light up. Each stage also lists the charting tools that help.

      The FOCUS stages: Find the opportunity (is it worth doing?), Observe the current state (how does it work now, and how well?), Create the plan (what will we change, who will do it, and when?), Undertake the change (deliver, track and report) and Sustain the benefit (keep the gains and hand over). Small projects need fewer documents: a scoping page, a baseline and an action log may be enough. Use the rest as your work grows.

      The worked example: every example is filled in for the same made-up programme, , so you can follow information from one document to the next: the support plan's deliverables become the highlight report's, risks in the RAID log appear in the leadership report, and the benefits register's baselines come back in the closure report. All names, figures and dates are made up.

      Measuring improvement: the documents say what you will measure; the FOCUS charting tools help you show it. Use a run chart or SPC chart for baselines and progress, a driver diagram to link your aim to your workstreams, and the A3 report to tell the story on a page.

      Templates: the blank templates are the CITI programme templates (version October 2022). Download them here or from your CITI team. Each document's page also gives its CITI template letter, such as F for the RAID log.

      Want help running a programme? Book QI training with the CITI Academy or talk to the QI Coach chatbot.
