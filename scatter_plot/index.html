Build a scatter plot

Paste pairs of measurements and see whether one moves with the other. Everything runs in your browser, so your data is never uploaded.

Your data

Two columns of numbers: the factor you think drives the change (X), then the result (Y). You can add a label column before them and a group column after them.

Chart settings

Result

Paste data on the left to build your chart.

How to read it: each dot is one pair of observations. Put the factor you are trying to change on the horizontal axis and the result you expect to respond on the vertical axis. If the dots rise from left to right, that's a positive correlation; if they fall, it's negative; if they are scattered all over, there's no evidence of a relationship.

Before you act: a correlation can suggest cause and effect, but it doesn't prove it. If an SPC chart shows a shift in either measure, plot the stable and changed periods as separate groups rather than mixing them in one cloud of points.
