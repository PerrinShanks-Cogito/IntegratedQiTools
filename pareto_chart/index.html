Build a Pareto chart

Paste your data and see which few causes make up most of the problem. Everything runs in your browser, so your data is never uploaded.

Your data

Either two columns (category and count), or one column with a row for every occurrence — repeats are counted for you.

Chart settings

Group after: keep this many categories and put the rest into "Other". Use 0 to show them all.

Result

Paste data on the left to build your chart.

How to read it: bars show each category's count, largest first. The line shows the running total as a percentage. Categories up to the cut-off line are the "vital few", where improvement effort is likely to have most effect.
